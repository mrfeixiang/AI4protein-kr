PAPER OF THE WEEK · PEER REVIEWED

BindCraft: “one design, one binder” becomes a testable claim

이번 주 핵심 논문: 계산 설계가 대규모 스크리닝 의존도를 얼마나 줄였는가?

BindCraft uses AlphaFold2 weights as an optimization engine, generating sequence, backbone and interface together. Across 12 targets, the authors reported experimental success rates of 10 to 100%, averaging 46.3%, with predominantly nanomolar affinities.

InputTarget structure
GenerateBinder + interface
FilterAF2 + Rosetta metrics
DecideSPR and function

Read the small print: this is not “press one button and obtain a drug.” The workflow samples hundreds to thousands of trajectories, recommends at least 100 computationally filtered designs, and still relies on experimental testing.

MONDAY-MORNING LAB ACTIONS

Three changes worth making in an AIDD workflow

  1. 01

    Define experimental conversion before model ranking

    Choose the assay, acceptance threshold and negative controls before generating hundreds of candidates.

  2. 02

    Keep prediction, design and validation as separate columns

    A good structure score, predicted affinity and measured binding are different claims.

  3. 03

    Archive code, model version and selection logic

    Without provenance, a weekly radar becomes a list of names rather than a reproducible decision record.
